Good to know

Do you have vegetarian and Jain options?

Yes. Use the vegetarian filter on the menu, and tell us about Jain or other dietary needs when you book.

Can I adjust the spice level?

Always. Every dish is cooked to order, so ask for mild, medium or fiery.

Do I need to reserve a table?

We recommend it for dinner and weekends. Walk-ins are welcome when we have space.

Do you cater for large groups?

Yes, for private dining and outside events. Call us and we’ll plan a menu with you.
